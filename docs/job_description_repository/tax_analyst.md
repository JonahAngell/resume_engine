Job Summary:

Job Requirements
Disney Tax is a fast-paced, dynamic environment made up of multiple subgroups that collectively manage the tax needs of The Walt Disney Company and its Subsidiaries. Disney Tax Operations is a key component that provides team members with varied opportunities, which build skills and empower the tax professional of the future. This opportunity is a once in a career chance to participate as a change agent in a finance transformation. We want innovative team members who thrive on change and discovery.



About the Role & Program

The Analyst, Tax Data Mgmt & Innovation supports the execution, sustainment, and continuous improvement of Disney's tax technology and data strategy. This role operates at the intersection of tax, data, and technology and helps deliver practical, scalable solutions that improve data quality, reduce manual effort, and support transformation across Corporate Tax.

 

This position will provide hands-on support for key tax platforms and data processes, including ONESOURCE DataFlow, ONESOURCE Data Hub, ONESOURCE Income Tax, Power Platform solutions, dashboards, SharePoint, and related integrations. The Analyst will partner with Tax, Finance, IT, vendors, and TDMI team members to support system enhancements, testing, issue resolution, documentation, controls, and reporting needs.

 

What You Will Do

As a Tax Data Management & Innovation Analyst, you’ll have the opportunity to:

 

Assist with projects related to business and finance initiatives, prioritizing and managing a slate of overlapping tax requirements, gathering, documenting, and testing.
Support tax aspects of major business initiatives, including requirement planning, testing, communication & training.
Assist in the maintenance and support of ONESOURCE Income Tax, ONESOURCE DataFlow, and ONESOURCE Data Hub.
Build and optimize dashboards, reports, and automation solutions using Microsoft Power Apps and Microsoft Fabric to support tax compliance and data analysis. 
Provide support for project management and collaboration tools, including Workfront and SharePoint.
Maintain high levels of support by solving issues and responding to requests for data and reports.
Collaborate with team members and analyze data from multiple sources to identify trends, discrepancies, and opportunities for process improvement. 
Support ad hoc tasks related to special projects and tax technology initiatives. 
 

Required Qualifications & Skills

Curiosity about tax and technology
Proven problem-solving skills and strong attention to detail/accuracy
Self-starter with the ability to work independently
Strong time management and organizational skills
Excellent interpersonal skills
Demonstrated computer skills (e.g., Alteryx, Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, SQL, APIs, Python, Power Apps, Power BI, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric)
 

Required Education

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Information Systems, Data Science, Data Analytics, Computer Science, or a related field.
Preferred Qualifications

1+ years of relevant experience in tax, finance technology, data management, analytics, automation, or system support roles.
Prior experience with the ONESOURCE suite of software applications is a plus.
Hands-on experience with automation, reporting, or data tools such as Alteryx, Power Platform, Power BI, Python, SQL, SharePoint, Microsoft Fabric, or similar technologies.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to investigate data issues, document findings, and recommend practical resolutions.
