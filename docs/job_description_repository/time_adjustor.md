Job Summary:

About the Role & Team

The Walt Disney World Labor Attendance and Time Team are the people who make sure the hourly Cast Members at Walt Disney World Parks and Resorts have accurate time and attendance records – but the reality is much, much more. Using cutting-edge tech, we make sure that we are in compliance with all federal, state and Disney regulations.  We execute audits and design and implement new initiatives to streamline procedures for our Cast Members.  The team also helps enable accurate time and pay by assisting with questions from all levels of Cast. We are seeking a Time & Attendance Adjustor to join our team on an Internal Assignment for 12 months!


If you have natural curiosity, thrive in a collaborative environment and have confidence in your ability to learn and master new tech, please apply today! 

This is an hourly role reporting to the Walt Disney World Labor Attendance and Time Team, part of the Workforce Management family. 


This is a 12-month internal assignment opportunity without guarantee of permanent placement. This role is located in Lake Buena Vista, FL.


What You Will Do

With nearly 60,000 hourly Cast Members in Walt Disney World alone, making sure everyone gets paid accurately and on time is a huge, and critical function. Yes, we work to be in compliance with every regulation, but we take it a step further by mining the data we collect to provide meaningful, real-time insights into the business. Details are critical – a decimal point out of place can have massive impact – so we check and re-check our work and then help our teammates do the same. No two days as a Time and Attendance Adjustor are alike. On any given day, you might: 

Evaluate audits to ensure compliance and adherence to policies and that accurate time-entry and record cards have been submitted for the Walt Disney World hourly Cast Member base 
Research and test system/process concerns; intervene as necessary to resolve any accurate time recording, pay, or attendance issues 
Partner with professionals and stakeholders across The Walt Disney Company -- including Compensation, Labor Relations, Human Resources Information Systems (HRIS), Legal, Technology, Benefits, and Payroll -- to correct and resolve time and attendance issues 
Assist Cast Members and Leaders in the resolution of their time, pay, and attendance questions 
Research, analyze, and problem solve escalated time, pay, and attendance issues 
Learn new processes and software – our team is highly supportive of development and education
