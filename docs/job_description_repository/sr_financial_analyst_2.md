Disney Entertainment — Senior Financial Analyst
Disney Entertainment · Senior Financial Analyst

Job Summary:

Within The Walt Disney Company, Disney Entertainment (DE) is a business segment that brings together world-class product, technology, media and commercialization teams. DE manages the production and distribution of content via the Company’s streaming services (including Disney+, Hulu), broadcast and cable television networks, as well as content licensing (including theatrical, home entertainment, music, stage plays). DE Segment FP&A group acts as a key partner for DE Senior leadership and TWDC Corporate FP&A team to consolidate, analyze and report DE current and future financial strategy and global performance. The group also leads the sharing of best practices across DE Finance teams (lines of business, centers of excellence, international regions) for process improvements and works closely with Finance Transformation teams to constantly increase efficiencies.


 

Job Summary:

Manage and forecast the DE Segment Admin cost base, which has ~$50M annualized expense
Work with Accounting teams on quarter close related matters for DE Segment Admin
Consolidate the direct and indirect corporate allocations by partnering with Corporate FP&A and various DE business lines
Develop and analyze the segment’s MIBP / LTIC forecast, annual operating plan and long-range plan
Create executive level presentations of the segment’s international performance including regional income statements and cause of change commentary
Embrace finance transformation initiatives (including new EPM system implementation and testing)
 

Responsibilities and Duties of the Role:

3+ years of finance/accounting related work experience, preferably with a media/entertainment, technology, or start-up company

Proficient / Expert level in Excel and PowerPoint; and with financial reporting systems (e.g., Oracle EPM, COGNOS TM1)
Strong analytical skills

Ability to prioritize projects, handle tight deadlines, and achieve effective results in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
