Disney Experiences · Sr Technical Project Mgr

Job Summary:

At Disney Experiences Technology, our team creates world-class immersive digital experiences for the Company’s premier vacation brands including Disney’s Parks & Resorts worldwide, Disney Cruise Line, Aulani, A Disney Resort & Spa, and Disney Vacation Club. We are responsible for the end-to-end digital and physical Guest experience for all technology & digital-led initiatives across the Attractions & Entertainment, Food & Beverage, Resorts & Transportation, and Merchandise lines of business as well as other initiatives including the MyDisneyExperience app. 

As a Senior Technical Project Manager you will identify, plan and coordinate a set of related projects within a program and manage their interdependencies in support of specific business strategies. You will also apply project management principles and techniques to lead project schedule, scope, quality, cost and performance risks. The Senior Technical Project Manager will work closely with cross-discipline technology teams, leadership and business partners to understand technical and business direction, milestones and budget, as well as provide thought leadership to project management team. 

  

The Senior Technical Project Manager will report to the Manager, Technical Project Management. 

  

What You’ll Do   

Drives execution and delivery of technical projects through full delivery life cycle ensuring project is delivered timely and on-budget. 

Works with engineering teams, including Software Engineers, Product Managers, Architects, and leadership to establish processes across all program areas that will ensure success on strategically prioritized projects and improve efficiency. 

Defines, optimizes, and manages Agile project management ceremonies and processes for large project teams. Manage hardware/software procurement of assigned projects 

Develops a program plan comprised of parallel projects and multiple project managers that collectively produce work that has significant business impact 

Monitors & adjusts program plan to respond to unplanned changes, ensuring program is progressing to meet business goals 

Creates governance practices and provides senior-level leadership oversight and control for the program 

Implements fiscal practices and controls, authorizing, recording, and reporting on program expenditures & ROI 

Develops infrastructure needed to support the program 

  

Required Qualifications & Skills 

Minimum of 5 years of technical project/program management experience working with technical teams developing enterprise class solutions 

Understanding of Cloud, APIs, services, database technologies, technologist roles and responsibilities, capabilities & platform dependencies, software development lifecycle, engineering and/or product development 

Influence and negotiation, communication, professional maturity & organizational savvy, relationship building, dependency mapping 

Hands-on experience with practices such as Agile, Lean, Kanban, Waterfall as well as extensive experience with pragmatic blending of styles to facilitate collaboration and delivery 

Deep understanding of Agile technical practices, DevOps and testing concepts (CI/CD, TDD) 

Significant experience in coaching and mentoring teams on project management practices including Agile, Lean, Kanban, Waterfall 

Excellent ability to understand architectural and technical aspects of a project 

Experience managing large project budgets 

Experience with tools such as: Microsoft suite, Keynote, Smartsheet, MyPPM (Clarity), MyJira, Tableau, and PowerBI. 

  

Preferred Qualifications 

Project Management Professional (PMP) Certification 

Experience in supporting large-scale, global software development programs 

Agile Certifications ie CSM; ACP; CPO; SAFe; LeSS 

Required Education 

Bachelor’s degree in relevant field of study and/or equivalent work experience
