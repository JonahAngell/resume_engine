About the Role & Team

Through innovative storytelling and a culture of collaboration, Disney Live Entertainment creates, produces, and delivers remarkable and engaging entertainment experiences. From the intimate to the spectacular, our work can be seen at Disney theme parks, resort hotels, cruise ships, and other locations the world over. This diverse team – representing a wide variety of disciplines and talents from technical directors, writers and lighting designers to choreographers, cosmetologists, and music producers – brings magical worlds to life through technical expertise, performance excellence, incomparable ingenuity, unparalleled spectacle… and a dash of pixie dust.

The Costuming Project Management Office (PMO) aligns project outcomes with Disney Live Entertainment's strategic business objectives. While our clients produce highly-crafted and innovative costumes that enhance Disney storytelling, we create our own form of magic by helping teams navigate complex challenges, improve the way work gets done, and transform ideas into results. The Business Analyst supports this mission by partnering with stakeholders to understand business needs, evaluate opportunities, and define solutions that drive informed decision-making and continuous improvement.

If selected for this position, you will report to the Senior Manager - Projects & Initiatives.

This is a Casual Temporary/Seasonal role with no guarantee of weekly working hours, as hours will fluctuate based on business needs.

What You Will Do

Support costuming with new business processes, feasibility, and business case definition
Gather requirements for various workflows
Perform business process modeling
Provide business change management support
Ensure work is aligning to business standards
Provide project support
Run reporting and create automation from multiple sources
Required Qualifications & Skills

Minimum two years of experience working on project teams
Experience with hands on report development and sustainment utilizing business intelligence tools such as Tableau, Power BI, Smartsheet or SQL
Skilled in project management tools such as Jira, Confluence, or MS Project
Prior experience translating complex data and analytics into compelling business stories
Ability to collaborate and work through compromises that result in win/win agreements and outcomes
Strong communication skills, with the ability to effectively engage partners at all levels both internally and externally
Experience working in a fast-paced environment with competing tasks
Willing to travel between various work locations in a given day
Ability to work around all types of fibers, fabrics, and synthetic furs
Schedule availability to work flexible hours including weekends, nights, and holidays
Ability to travel domestically & internationally up to 10% of the time
Preferred Qualifications

Experience with Walt Disney Parks and Resorts Costuming and/or products
Education

High School Diploma or equivalent required
